# Brand Name Options (Q-01)

What the name needs to do:
- Short, easy to say and spell in **both Arabic and English** (and later Kurdish).
- Feels local and trustworthy, not like a foreign franchise.
- Not tied to one city, so it works nationally including Kurdistan.
- Available as a domain (`.iq` and/or `.com`) and on social media.
- Distinct from existing brands (Property Finder, Bayut, OpenSooq, Aqar, Dubizzle).

> ⚠️ Claude cannot check domain, trademark or social-handle availability. Please check the shortlisted names before deciding.

## Shortlist

| # | Name | Arabic | Meaning & rationale | Notes |
|---|------|--------|--------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Mahalla** | محلّة | "Neighbourhood". In Iraq the *mahalla* is the core unit of an address and of identity. Very local, warm, and says "find your place". | Distinctly Iraqi usage. `mahalla.iq` |
| 2 | **Tabu** | طابو | The word Iraqis use for a registered title deed. Signals "real, legal, trustworthy property", which is our differentiator. Short and catchy in both languages. | May feel technical to some users. Check Turkish "Tapu" brand conflicts. |
| 3 | **Beitna** | بيتنا | "Our home". Emotional, family-oriented, easy for everyone. | Common phrase, so may be harder to trademark. |
| 4 | **Dijla** | دجلة | The Tigris river: rooted in Baghdad and Mesopotamian identity. Elegant, premium. | Used by other Iraqi businesses (check conflicts). Less obvious it's real estate, so would need a tagline. |
| 5 | **Sakan** | سكن | "Housing / residence". Clear meaning, simple. | Generic, so domain likely taken. |
| 6 | **Daar** | دار | "House / home". One syllable, classic, premium. | Very generic; many "Dar…" brands across the region. |

## Claude's recommendation

**Mahalla (محلّة)** first, **Tabu (طابو)** second.

- *Mahalla* is unmistakably Iraqi, not generic, emotionally positive, and connects directly to how Iraqis search ("which mahalla?"). It also gives a natural tagline: *"لكل بيت محلّة" / "Every home has a mahalla."*
- *Tabu* is the stronger trust message but slightly colder.

The name can be changed later in configuration until the logo is finalized: no code depends on it.
